# Code of Conduct

- Be respectful. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Credit other people's work. Don't pass off someone else's code, patches or
  builds as your own.
- Keep discussions on topic and assume good faith.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and
ban people who don't follow this. To report a problem, contact a maintainer
listed in [AUTHORS](AUTHORS) privately.
